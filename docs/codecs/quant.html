quant

Uniform steps in a warped domain.

A quantizer that respects a pointwise bound b(x) is a uniform quantizer in a domain w with w′ = 1/b, so the curve is not a free choice, it follows from the way the measurement error of the sensor grows with the value. linear holds a flat bound and fits elevation or temperature, sqrt one that grows as √x and fits photon counting, log a relative one and fits SAR backscatter or concentrations. The encoder does not argue the bound. It decodes what it just wrote, measures every sample against what the recipe declared, and refuses the frame instead of writing one that misses. A near-lossless codec sits once at the head of the graph, so the loss happens a single time and every stage after it stays exact.
