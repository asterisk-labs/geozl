quant_linear

A uniform grid, anchored at zero.

An absolute bound. |x − x̂| ≤ V puts the levels on a uniform grid of step 2V, anchored at zero, floored when the step must be an integer. Integer input, or floats already on a whole-number grid, store the reconstruction and the decoder copies or casts. Anything else stores the index and the decoder multiplies by the step. Every sample is verified against the bound before the frame is written.
