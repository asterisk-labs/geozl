quant_sqrt

Wider slack where the noise is wider.

A bound shaped by the sensor. Shot noise grows with the square root of the signal, so a bright sample gets more room than a dark one and every sample is held to the same fraction of its own noise. The levels are uniform in u = √(x + offset), which puts them at (q · step)² − offset and leaves the whole decode as two multiplies and a subtract. The encoder takes the level nearest the sample in x rather than in u, because the cell boundaries are not the midpoints there, and the difference is worth a third of the step. MAX_ERROR counts sigmas of σ² = a + b·x, a curve the recipe either carries or measures off the raster, and a measured one reports four numbers saying how far to trust it. Fit it once over the product, since two tiles fitted separately land on different grids; geozl.lossy.fit_noise pools a stack into one curve.
