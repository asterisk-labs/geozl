quant_sqrt

Wider slack where the noise is wider.

A noise-proportional bound. MAX_ERROR counts sigmas of the sensor noise σ² = a + b·x. The levels are uniform in u = √(x + offset), so level q reconstructs as (q · step)² − offset, two multiplies and a subtract. The recipe either carries a and b or fits them from the raster. Fit the curve once over the whole product with geozl.lossy.fit_noise, since two tiles fitted separately land on different grids. The bound applies where a + b·x > 0; non-finite samples belong in nodata.
