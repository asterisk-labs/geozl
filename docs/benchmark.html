benchmark

Ratio and speed.

How geozl trades compression ratio against encode and decode throughput, across raster types.

One raster is measured today, a 512 × 512 int16 window of the Copernicus DEM, with every applicable recipe timed and drawn against its pareto front on the Python API page.

What is missing is the sweep across raster families rather than one tile. DEM, Sentinel-2 L1C and Sentinel-1 RTC do not rank the recipes the same way, so one scene says nothing about the next. Until those are measured on hardware worth quoting, run geozl.profile on a tile of your own.
